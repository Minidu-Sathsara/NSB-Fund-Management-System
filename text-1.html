Welcome To Piliyandala Central College

Welcome to the Piliyandala Central College, a shining example of scholastic brilliance located in the heart of Piliyandala. Our college is one of the best colleges in UP which is ensuring academic excellence, character development and all-round development of each student. Infusing the best of tradition and innovation to educate future leaders in academics, sports, arts, and community service with a proud half-century legacy. We express diversity, foster curiosity and provide students with an environment to become the best they can at Piliyandala Central College.

Our School facilities

We are proud to offer facilities that support learning and creativity while fostering development. Our campus features a library and advanced computer and science labs alongside recreational zones and well appointed classrooms. Students can enjoy our sports fields and playgrounds, for education and physical exercise. Our facilities prioritize safety and comfort with design to create an environment, for your childs growth.
